About NF

Nathan Feuerstein aka NF, is a rapper who is known for his emotionally raw and dense lyrical music. His career started with Christian music circles, but has since then achieved mainstream success with his albums "Perception" and "The Search". Even with this amount of success NF lives a very private life choosing not to do interviews and decides to communicate through his music.

NF's music is themed around his childhood trauma, His mother died when he was 18 due to an overdose and lived through abuse from his mom's boyfriend, these trauma situations are a central theme in his music. His early influences were Christian rappers such as "Lecrae", and "Andy Mineo", these influences later expanded to other artists such as "Ed Sheeran", and "Adele".
